## 15-day plan to reach 100–200 people
- Days 1–2: Make a list of 100 people you know who fit. Finish the landing page and test the link on a phone.
- Days 3–5: Send the personal invite below to 20 people per day. Reply to everyone who answers.
- Days 6–8: Post 1 useful tip per day on [LinkedIn / Instagram / Facebook] with the page link in the bio.
- Days 9–11: Share in 5–8 relevant groups where it is allowed. Give value first, then the link.
- Days 12–13: Ask early members to forward the invite to one friend.
- Days 14–15: Remind everyone about the launch. Check how many joined and where they came from.

## Nurture plan (before launch on [date], [time])
- Day of joining: Welcome message. Say what the group is for and the rules.
- Daily: One short tip or example (text or image). Keep it under 100 words.
- Every 3 days: Ask one easy question so people reply.
- 3 days before: "Something special is coming on [date] at [time]." Share what will happen.
- 1 day before: Reminder with the plan for the session.
- Launch morning: "We start in 1 hour." Send the link.
- After: Share a recap for people who missed it.
Keep urgency honest: real dates, real limits only.
